Your integration works — except when it matters. Orders don't reach the ERP, stock doesn't match, and nobody knows why. We've seen it. We orchestrate heterogeneous systems using real Enterprise Integration Patterns, not Visio diagrams.

## Connector ≠ integrated system

Having a connector doesn't mean your systems talk to each other. We design architectures that bridge your transactional core with cloud ecosystems, closing the data loop between operational and analytical systems.

- **Apache Camel** on Karaf or Quarkus: code-first integration, 300+ connectors, full control over mediation and transformation
- **Async messaging**: Kafka, Pub/Sub or ActiveMQ Artemis as a decoupled communication backbone
- **Cloud native**: Dataflow, Cloud Functions or Cloudflare Workers for edge integration logic
- **Governed APIs**: Contract First design, secured with `OAuth 2.0` and `mTLS` from day one
