Your Cloudflare is configured. Are you sure it's configured right? We know why those WAF rules are there. We know which Workers run business logic and which ones are dead weight. We design, deploy and maintain edge security architectures in production.

## WAF ≠ security

A firewall is not a security strategy. We configure Cloudflare as architecture, not as a checkbox:

- **Custom WAF**: rules tuned to your actual attack surface, not generic templates. OWASP Top 10, DDoS, bots
- **Zero Trust**: access to internal apps without VPN, with identity and device-verified policies
- **Workers**: business logic running at the edge, under 50ms from the user. This isn't a CDN — it's your code running on 300+ PoPs
- **CDN and caching**: static content served from Cloudflare's global network. Less origin load, predictable response times

## Who configures it, maintains it

We don't set up Cloudflare and disappear. We monitor traffic, adjust rules when attack patterns change, and respond when something degrades. Three engineers who know your configuration — not a ticket to a generic partner.
