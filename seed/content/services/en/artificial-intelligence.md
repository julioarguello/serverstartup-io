Most AI projects die at the demo — a chatbot that can't touch the order, a copilot that can't see the stock. We come at it from the other side: systems first, agent second.

## AI with its hands in your system

Fifteen-plus years integrating ERPs, catalogues and data platforms left us with something better than a model: the map. A useful agent needs to read the order in SAP, check stock in BigQuery and write the result where your team actually works. That is integration — and integration is home ground for us.

We are building this practice under the same rule as the rest: depth before breadth, and nothing in production we cannot explain line by line.

This page will grow as there are cases worth telling. That is how we work.
