Privacy policy
We process very little data, and we explain it in a few lines. This policy is governed by Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and by Spain's Organic Law 3/2018 (LOPDGDD).
Data controller
SERVER STARTUP, S.L. Tax ID B95944807. Address: C/ Los Fueros 11 — 48992 Getxo (Bizkaia). For anything concerning your data, write to legal@serverstartup.io.
What we process
Whatever you send us by email or WhatsApp: your address, your name if you sign it, and what you tell us. This site has no forms, no registration and no user accounts.
Why we process it
To answer your enquiry and, if we end up working together, to manage the contractual relationship. We do not use your data for advertising and we do not add you to mailing lists.
Legal basis
Your consent when you contact us and, where a professional relationship exists, performance of a contract and compliance with the associated legal obligations.
You may withdraw your consent at any time by writing to us, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before the withdrawal.
Recipients
We do not share your data with third parties unless the law requires it. We do rely on technology providers — email, hosting and site protection — which act as data processors, handle the data only on our instructions, and are bound by a processing agreement.
International transfers
Some of those providers are established outside the European Economic Area. Where that entails an international transfer, it relies on a European Commission adequacy decision or on standard contractual clauses, with any additional safeguards required.
Retention
We keep correspondence for as long as it takes to deal with it and, where invoicing is involved, for the periods required by commercial and tax law. After that we delete it.
Your rights
You may exercise your rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, portability and objection by writing to the address above. If you believe your request has not been handled properly, you may lodge a complaint with the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).
Cookies
This site uses no tracking or advertising cookies, and sets no cookie that would require your consent. The analytics we use work without cookies and without identifying you.
Changes to this policy
If the way we process data changes, we will update this page. The published version is always the one in force.
