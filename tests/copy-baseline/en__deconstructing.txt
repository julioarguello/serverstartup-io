Deconstructing this website

The architecture
Astro with server-side rendering on Cloudflare Workers. Content lives in EmDash, a CMS running in the same Worker, with the database on D1 and images on R2. No servers to babysit. The page you are reading was served from a point of presence less than 50 ms away from you.
Content as code
Every text on this site lives in seed/, versioned in Git. A clean rebuild reproduces the whole site, word for word. When a text changes, there is a commit that tells the story.
The agents
The first draft of every text is written by an agent with a copywriting skill. The house voice, a blacklist of forbidden words, and an AI-tell detector with a score. Then a human reads it, corrects it and signs it. The review step is not optional. It is the product.
The numbers
Lighthouse 100 in performance, accessibility, best practices and SEO across all four templates, measured on the production build on August 6, 2026. W3C validation, zero errors. LCP under 1.3 seconds on mobile. When these change, this line changes.
The workshop, in plain sight
Code, seeds, skills and prompts live in the repository. Look inside and tell us what you would do better. Probably something. That is how good conversations start.
